# Service Level Agreement Terms (2023)

**Effective:** 2023-01-01. **Status:** superseded on 2024-07-01 by the 2024 terms.

## Availability commitment

RAGBench Mutual commits to 99.5 percent monthly availability for HarborShield AI, ClaimPilot, and Aurora Risk Suite.

## Service credits

If monthly availability falls below 99.5 percent, the customer receives a service credit of 5 percent of that month's fees. There is a single credit tier.

## Support response

Severity 1 issues require customer acknowledgement within 30 minutes. Severity 2 issues require acknowledgement within 4 business hours.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 business days ahead does not count against availability.
