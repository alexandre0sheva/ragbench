# Support Coverage Matrix

Which regions get which hours, languages, and support tier. Hours are local to the region's primary support hub.

| Region | Hub | Coverage hours | Languages | Highest tier available |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North America | Chicago | 24x7 | English, Spanish | Premium |
| Latin America | Mexico City | Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 | Spanish, Portuguese, English | Standard |
| Europe | Dublin | 24x7 | English, German, French | Premium |
| Middle East and Africa | Dublin | Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 | English, French | Standard |
| Asia Pacific | Singapore | Monday to Saturday, 07:00 to 19:00 | English, Mandarin, Japanese | Premium |

## Tier definitions

**Standard** support includes the ticket portal and email, with first response within one business day.

**Premium** support adds the 24x7 phone line and named-engineer escalation; it requires the Premium Support add-on.

## Holidays

Regional hubs observe local public holidays. During a holiday the hub runs on a reduced roster, and Severity 1 issues are routed to the nearest 24x7 hub.
