# Compliance Bulletin 2024-06

**Published:** 2024-06-28. **Effective:** 2024-07-15. **Author:** Naomi Fischer, Head of Compliance.

This bulletin updates the retention rule in Compliance Bulletin 2023-11. Source citations for every generated underwriting or claims recommendation must now be preserved for at least 24 months, up from 18 months. The rule applies to HarborShield AI, ClaimPilot, and Aurora Risk Suite.

Customers may request an export of the citations attached to their own recommendations; the export must be delivered within 10 business days of the request.

All other provisions of Bulletin 2023-11 remain in force, including the requirement that AI outputs are advisory and must not be described as final coverage, pricing, or claim payment decisions.
