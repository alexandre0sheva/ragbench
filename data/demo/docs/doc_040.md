# Aurora Risk Suite: Team Plan Sheet

Aurora Risk Suite Team is the entry-level plan for portfolio analytics. This sheet lists what the plan includes.

## Pricing and seats

- Price: $89 per seat per month, billed annually.
- Minimum seats: 5.
- Maximum seats: 50.

## Workspace limits

- Scenario Board scenarios per workspace: 10.
- Export row cap: 25,000 rows per XLSX export.
- Data retention: 12 months.

## Support

- Support response time: 8 business hours.
- Single sign-on: not included; available as an add-on.

Plans can be changed at renewal. Usage above a plan's limits is blocked rather than billed; an export that exceeds the row cap fails with error code AR-3308.
