# Remote Work Policy, Version 3.0

**Effective:** 2024-04-01. **Status:** current. Replaces Version 2.0.

This policy sets out where and how RAGBench Mutual employees work.

## Office attendance

Employees are expected in the office two days per week. The other three days may be worked from home without special approval. Teams choose their anchor days together.

## Home office support

New remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $450, paid with their first payroll. All remote-eligible employees also receive a $40 monthly internet reimbursement.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to 20 days per calendar year, with manager approval and a security check of the destination.

## Equipment

The company supplies a laptop. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data.
