# Aurora Risk Suite: Business Plan Sheet

Aurora Risk Suite Business is the mid-tier plan for portfolio analytics. This sheet lists what the plan includes.

## Pricing and seats

- Price: $119 per seat per month, billed annually.
- Minimum seats: 10.
- Maximum seats: 250.

## Workspace limits

- Scenario Board scenarios per workspace: 25.
- Export row cap: 100,000 rows per XLSX export.
- Data retention: 36 months.

## Support

- Support response time: 4 business hours.
- Single sign-on: included.

Plans can be changed at renewal. Usage above a plan's limits is blocked rather than billed; an export that exceeds the row cap fails with error code AR-3308.
