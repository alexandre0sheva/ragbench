# The Harbor Light: Customer Ops Newsletter, April 2024

Hey everyone! Another month, another pile of tickets conquered. Grab a coffee (the third-floor machine finally does oat milk, you are welcome) and skim what's new.

## Big news: we moved!

Customer Operations is now on the fourth floor. The move happened on 2024-04-22, a Monday, and yes, the plants survived. The old third-floor bay is being turned into a quiet room. Please do not take calls in there; that is the whole point of a quiet room.

## Shout-outs

- Carla Mendes and the support crew cleared the entire backlog of "where is my export?" tickets in two weeks. Legends.
- Priya Desai got a lovely note from Meridian Underwriters about the HarborShield pilot wrap-up.
- Elias Ren's reliability team has been writing the new autoscaling playbook. Thank him by not breaking the extraction workers.

## Fun numbers

The team answered 2,318 tickets in March. That is 9 percent fewer than February, mostly because the new Broker Portal FAQ is doing its job. Median first reply time was 27 minutes, which is comfortably inside the 30-minute Severity 1 target even though most tickets are not Severity 1.

## Watch this space

There is a rumor that the Product Council is meeting on 2024-04-16 to lock in dates for the next two releases. No promises, but keep an eye on your calendar invites.

## Office trivia

The fourth-floor kitchen has a snack drawer. The snack drawer is labeled "Snacks (emergency only)". Nobody has worked out what counts as an emergency.

Until next month: be kind, reply to your tickets, and never sit on a file.
