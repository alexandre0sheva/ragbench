# Cascadia Re Case Study

Cascadia Re is a reinsurer that adopted Aurora Risk Suite Enterprise in May 2024 under contract MSA-8244, with 120 seats.

Before moving to Enterprise, Cascadia Re ran a pilot on the Team plan, where its portfolio exports failed with error code AR-3308 because they were larger than the plan's row cap. After the move, its quarterly reporting cycle shortened from 9 days to 3 days.

Cascadia's analysts rely on the Scenario Board to compare pricing assumptions across treaty years, and asked for better side-by-side views of renewal exposure.
