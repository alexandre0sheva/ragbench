# Incident Report INC-2024-0919: Database Failover Promoted a Lagging Replica

**Severity:** 1. **Date:** 2024-09-19. **Owner:** Hannah Voss.

## Timeline

The incident began at 11:15 UTC and ended at 13:25 UTC, a duration of 130 minutes. Aurora Risk Suite dashboards were unavailable for the whole period.

## Impact

41 customers were affected. Aurora availability for September 2024 was 99.70 percent. About 6 minutes of Scenario Board edits made immediately before the failover were lost.

## Cause

An automatic failover promoted a replica whose lag was far behind the primary. The replica lag alert threshold at the time was 120 seconds, too loose to have warned the team before the failover.

## Corrective actions

- Tighten the replica lag alert threshold to 30 seconds. Owner: Elias Ren.
- Require incident commander approval before promoting any replica above the lag threshold. Owner: Hannah Voss.
