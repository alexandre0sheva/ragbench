# Remote Work Policy, Version 2.0

**Effective:** 2023-06-01. **Status:** superseded on 2024-04-01 by Version 3.0. Replaces Version 1.0.

This policy sets out where and how RAGBench Mutual employees work.

## Office attendance

Employees are expected in the office three days per week. The other two days may be worked from home without special approval.

## Home office support

New remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $300, paid with their first payroll.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to 10 days per calendar year, with manager approval and a security check of the destination.

## Equipment

The company supplies a laptop. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data.
