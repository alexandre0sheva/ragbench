# Incident Report INC-2024-0412: Expired Certificate on Extraction Ingress

**Severity:** 1. **Date:** 2024-04-12. **Owner:** Dara Okafor.

## Timeline

The incident began at 14:02 UTC and ended at 15:22 UTC, a duration of 80 minutes. An internal certificate on the document extraction ingress expired, and uploads to the Broker Portal were rejected until a new certificate was issued and deployed.

## Impact

19 customers were unable to upload packets during the incident. HarborShield AI reviews already in progress finished normally. No customer data was lost.

## Cause

The certificate was not covered by the certificate expiry alert, which at the time fired only 7 days before expiry and only for customer-facing certificates.

## Corrective actions

- Extend expiry alerts to internal certificates. Owner: Dara Okafor.
- Alert 45 days, 14 days, and 3 days before expiry. Owner: Elias Ren.
