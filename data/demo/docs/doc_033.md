# Remote Work Policy, Version 1.0

**Effective:** 2022-03-01. **Status:** superseded on 2023-06-01 by Version 2.0.

This policy sets out where and how RAGBench Mutual employees work.

## Office attendance

Employees are expected in the office four days per week. The remaining day may be worked from home with manager approval.

## Home office support

The company does not provide a home office stipend under this version of the policy. Employees may borrow a monitor from the office for the duration of their employment.

## Working from abroad

Working from outside the country of employment is not permitted under this version.

## Equipment

The company supplies a laptop. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data.
