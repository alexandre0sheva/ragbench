# Lakeshore Mutual Case Study

Lakeshore Mutual adopted ClaimPilot for commercial property claims on 2024-06-03 under contract MSA-8190. About 380 adjusters use it.

In the first ten weeks, Lakeshore's misrouting rate for litigation claims fell from 14 percent to 6 percent, and average first-touch time fell from 95 minutes to 52 minutes.

Grace Holloway, Lakeshore's VP of Claims, said the evidence checklist was the feature her adjusters used most. Lakeshore asked for a dashboard showing how long claims have sat in each queue.
