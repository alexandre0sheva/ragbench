# Service Level Agreement Terms (2024)

**Effective:** 2024-07-01. **Status:** current. Replaces the 2023 terms.

## Availability commitment

RAGBench Mutual commits to 99.9 percent monthly availability for HarborShield AI, ClaimPilot, and Aurora Risk Suite.

## Service credits

| Monthly availability | Service credit |
| --- | --- |
| Below 99.9 percent but at least 99.5 percent | 10 percent of that month's fees |
| Below 99.5 percent | 25 percent of that month's fees |

## Support response

Severity 1 issues require customer acknowledgement within 30 minutes. Severity 2 issues require acknowledgement within 4 business hours.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 business days ahead does not count against availability.
