# HarborShield AI: Atlantic Coverage Pack

The Atlantic Coverage Pack extends HarborShield AI with port, route, and sanctions data for the Atlantic trade lanes. This sheet lists what the pack includes.

## Coverage

- Ports covered: 142.
- Packets included per month: 400.
- Sanctions list refresh: every 6 hours.

## Pricing

- Pack price: $1,450 per month.
- Overage price: $3.10 per packet above the monthly allowance.

The pack is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time before the next billing date. Packets beyond the allowance are processed as normal and charged at the overage price.
