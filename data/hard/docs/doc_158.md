# Orlan Glassworks: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 31 May 2022.*

Orlan Glassworks is a maker of optical glass and lens assemblies. It was founded in 1991 in Ferrisvale, where its head office is located. Ivo Calloway founded Orlan and served as its first chief executive. Orlan describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. It files its annual return with the register on time. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales.
