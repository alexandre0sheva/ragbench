# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q1 2024

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the first quarter of 2024.

Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms. The company joined an industry working group on shared standards for packaging waste. Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. Maintenance at the coastal test site was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. In May, the quality group completed a review of digital service records at the training rooms.

The quality group repeated its check of customer onboarding and closed all open actions. Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the service centre was replaced.

The board discussed warehouse layout at 33 of its meetings. The board discussed shift planning at 39 of its meetings. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. The company published a revised supplier handbook in March. A new scheduling tool now plans work at the training rooms a week ahead.

The management team met customers at 39 regional trade events.
