# Pellucid Optical: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 17 March 2022.*

Pellucid Optical is a chain of optician shops. It was founded in 2006 in Corvisk, where its head office is located. Jolien Ulvaeus founded the business. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Pellucid Optical describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The register lists Pellucid Optical as a private limited company.
