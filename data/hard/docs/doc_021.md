# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q2 2024

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the second quarter of 2024.

Internal audit looked at document control and reported no significant findings. The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. The company continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. Changes to returns handling were rolled out in stages to limit disruption.

Changes to spare-parts planning were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in February.
