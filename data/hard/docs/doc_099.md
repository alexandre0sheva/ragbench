# Sablewood Logistics completes acquisition of Zephyrine Cables

Sablewood Logistics completed its acquisition of Zephyrine Cables on 15 December 2024, after approval by the Meridian Coast Competition Office. Zephyrine Cables keeps its name and its own management team. The parties did not disclose the price.

Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. The group reviewed its approach to digital service records during the year and simplified several internal steps.
