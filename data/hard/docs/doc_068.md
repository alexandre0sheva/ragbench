# Ashgrove Analytics appoints Uriel Vandermeer as chief executive

Ashgrove Analytics announced on 29 January 2022 that its board had appointed Uriel Vandermeer as chief executive officer with effect from 10 March 2022. Uriel Vandermeer succeeds Florian Thackeray, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Uriel Vandermeer was previously managing director of the export business at Lumen Harbor Systems.

The management team met customers at 3 regional trade events. A new scheduling tool now plans work at the main workshop a week ahead.
