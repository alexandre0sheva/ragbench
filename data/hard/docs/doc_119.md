# Hessle Motorworks appoints Mireille Gallagher as chief executive

Hessle Motorworks announced on 26 September 2019 that its board had appointed Mireille Gallagher as chief executive officer with effect from 18 October 2019. Mireille Gallagher succeeds Ragna Okafor, who is retiring. Mireille Gallagher was previously managing director of the export business at Ironvale Sensors.

Maintenance at the service centre was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in January.
