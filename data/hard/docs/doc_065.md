# Umbra Grid appoints Ximena Gunnarsson as chief executive

Umbra Grid announced on 29 December 2024 that its board had appointed Ximena Gunnarsson as chief executive officer with effect from 20 January 2025. Ximena Gunnarsson succeeds Nils Jessop, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Ximena Gunnarsson was previously chief operating officer at Orlan Glassworks.

Investment plans focus on packaging waste and on returns handling. Umbra reviewed its approach to warehouse layout during the year and simplified several internal steps.
