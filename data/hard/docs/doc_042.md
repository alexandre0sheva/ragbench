# Lumen Harbor Systems: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 3 August 2022.*

Lumen Harbor Systems is a developer of port management software. It was founded in 2014 in Halbrook, where its head office is located. Brigid Abernathy founded Lumen Harbor and served as its first chief executive. Lumen Harbor takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop.
