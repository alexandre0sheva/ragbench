# Zephyrine Cables: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 4 October 2022.*

Zephyrine Cables is a maker of subsea tether cables. It was founded in 1990 in Halbrook, where its head office is located. Bertil Ivanova founded Zephyrine and served as its first chief executive. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Zephyrine takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring.
