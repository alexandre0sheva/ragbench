# Brightwater Instrumentation: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 30 September 2022.*

Brightwater Instrumentation is a distributor of laboratory instruments. It was founded in 1988 in Port Avalen, where its head office is located. Clemence Lowenthal founded the business. It files its annual return with the register on time. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The register lists Brightwater Instrumentation as a private limited company.
