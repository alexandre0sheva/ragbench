# Port Avalen harbour tariff, 2022 edition

Issued by the Port Avalen Harbour Board. This edition takes effect on 1 July 2022 and replaces the edition of 1 January 2021.

| Charge | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Berth fee, per metre of length per day | MC 2.81 |
| Pilotage, per movement | MC 437 |
| Container handling, per TEU | MC 53 |
| Waste reception, per call | MC 130 |

Charges are invoiced to the ship's agent after departure. Vessels on scheduled ferry services pay under a separate agreement. Disputes about an invoice must be raised within thirty days.
