# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q4 2025

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the fourth quarter of 2025.

A pilot of document control at the training rooms ran from February to September and will be extended. Norrland continued to invest in the assembly hall, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The company continued to invest in the northern depot, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms. The company sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty.

Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. Investment plans focus on energy metering and on workshop safety.

In May, the training unit completed a review of supplier audits at the main workshop. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform. The group reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers. The quality group repeated its check of document control and closed all open actions. The quality group repeated its check of warehouse layout and closed all open actions.

Work on customer onboarding continued, with most of the planned changes now in place.
