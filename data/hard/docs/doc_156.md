# Sablewood Logistics: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 18 September 2022.*

Sablewood Logistics is a warehousing and logistics group. It was founded in 1992 in Merrowgate, where its head office is located. Piet Halloway founded Sablewood and served as its first chief executive. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. It files its annual return with the register on time. Sablewood describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. The register lists Sablewood as a private limited company.
