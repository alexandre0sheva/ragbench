# Brightwater Instruments: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 10 November 2022.*

Brightwater Instruments is a maker of marine survey instruments. It was founded in 1999 in Corvisk, where its head office is located. Percival Kerrigan founded Brightwater and served as its first chief executive. The register lists Brightwater as a private limited company. Brightwater describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. It files its annual return with the register on time.
