# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q1 2025

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the first quarter of 2025.

Work on calibration routines continued, with most of the planned changes now in place. Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. A new scheduling tool now plans work at the northern depot a week ahead. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. Norrland reviewed its approach to customer onboarding during the year and simplified several internal steps.

Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the main workshop was replaced. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.

Norrland reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers. Maintenance at the service centre was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. The facilities team introduced a shorter checklist for spare-parts planning, which reduced rework. The group updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. The company sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty.

Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules.
