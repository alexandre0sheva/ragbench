# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q1 2024

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the first quarter of 2024.

Maintenance at the training rooms was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. Vantor continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. The group joined an industry working group on shared standards for warehouse layout. Vantor kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time.

The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms. Vantor published a revised supplier handbook in January. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. In April, the logistics desk completed a review of customer onboarding at the main workshop. Internal audit looked at warehouse layout and reported no significant findings.

The facilities team reported 39 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.
