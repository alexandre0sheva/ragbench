# Calder Freight completes acquisition of Redfern Composites

Calder Freight completed its acquisition of Redfern Composites on 1 December 2023, after approval by the Meridian Coast Competition Office. Redfern Composites keeps its name and its own management team. The parties did not disclose the price.

Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the training rooms was replaced. The company published a revised supplier handbook in January.
