# Lumen Harbor Systems completes acquisition of Pellucid Optics

Lumen Harbor Systems completed its acquisition of Pellucid Optics on 2 May 2025, after approval by the Meridian Coast Competition Office. Pellucid Optics keeps its name and its own management team. The parties did not disclose the price.

The company expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace. The company joined an industry working group on shared standards for workshop safety.
