# Marisco Devices: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 6 October 2022.*

Marisco Devices is a maker of sensors and tether cables. It was founded in 1995 in Merrowgate, where its head office is located. Pavel Whitlock founded Marisco and served as its first chief executive. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. Marisco takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme.
