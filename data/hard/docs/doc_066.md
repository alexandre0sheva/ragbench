# Quarry Point Robotics: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 6 June 2022.*

Quarry Point Robotics is a maker of dock and inspection robots. It was founded in 2012 in Halbrook, where its head office is located. Hamish Castellane founded Quarry Point and served as its first chief executive. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Quarry Point takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. It files its annual return with the register on time. Quarry Point describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies.
