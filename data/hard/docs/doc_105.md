# Quarry Point Robotics appoints Vivienne Montague as chief executive

Quarry Point Robotics announced on 30 March 2024 that its board had appointed Vivienne Montague as chief executive officer with effect from 20 April 2024. Vivienne Montague succeeds Hamish Castellane, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Vivienne Montague was previously finance director at Vantor Aerial.

A new scheduling tool now plans work at the training rooms a week ahead. The company continued to invest in the assembly hall, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair.
