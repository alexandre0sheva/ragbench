# Tessaline Maritime: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 7 September 2022.*

Tessaline Maritime is a ship-management agency. It was founded in 2013 in Ferrisvale, where its head office is located. Yolanda Esposito founded the business. Tessaline Maritime takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. It files its annual return with the register on time. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales.
