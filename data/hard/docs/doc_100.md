# Umbra Grid moves its head office

Umbra Grid completed the move of its head office from Ostrow Bay to Dunmore Reach on 12 March 2025. The former Ostrow Bay office will stay open as a regional service depot. Umbra ran 9 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality.

Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the assembly hall was replaced. Umbra expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.
