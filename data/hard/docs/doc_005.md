# Ashgrove Analytics completes acquisition of Ironvale Sensors

Ashgrove Analytics completed its acquisition of Ironvale Sensors on 2 September 2023, after approval by the Meridian Coast Competition Office. Ironvale Sensors keeps its name and its own management team. The parties did not disclose the price.

Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. A pilot of customer onboarding at the northern depot ran from May to October and will be extended.
