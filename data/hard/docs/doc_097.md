# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q2 2025

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the second quarter of 2025.

The group reviewed its approach to digital service records during the year and simplified several internal steps. The company ran 23 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. The quality group repeated its check of document control and closed all open actions. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in April. The group reviewed its approach to energy metering during the year and simplified several internal steps.

The group continued to invest in the northern depot, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. Investment plans focus on digital service records and on customer onboarding.
