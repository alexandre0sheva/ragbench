# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q3 2024

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the third quarter of 2024.

Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. Spare-parts availability improved after the field service team reorganised the coastal test site. The group reviewed its approach to document control during the year and simplified several internal steps. The group continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. The field service team reported 30 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.

The company joined an industry working group on shared standards for calibration routines. Internal audit looked at shift planning and reported no significant findings.
