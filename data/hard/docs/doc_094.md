# Ironvale Sensors: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 8 November 2022.*

Ironvale Sensors is a maker of pressure sensors. It was founded in 2009 in Saltmere, where its head office is located. Kaia Nyberg founded Ironvale and served as its first chief executive. Ironvale takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. It files its annual return with the register on time.
