# Kestova Energy: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 17 September 2022.*

Kestova Energy is a maker of battery packs and chargers. It was founded in 1997 in Tirlan, where its head office is located. Vesna Holmqvist founded Kestova and served as its first chief executive. Kestova takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. The register lists Kestova as a private limited company. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop.
