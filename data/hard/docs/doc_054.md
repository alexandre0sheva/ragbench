# Calder Freight moves its head office

Calder Freight completed the move of its head office from Ferrisvale to Dunmore Reach on 8 February 2025. The former Ferrisvale office will stay open as a regional service depot. Calder joined an industry working group on shared standards for energy metering.

The group published a revised supplier handbook in June. The board discussed calibration routines at 12 of its meetings.
