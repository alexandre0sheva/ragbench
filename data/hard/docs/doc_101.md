# Yarrow Drive Systems: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 15 October 2022.*

Yarrow Drive Systems is a maker of conveyor drive systems. It was founded in 2011 in Ostrow Bay, where its head office is located. Soren Imrie founded the business. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. Yarrow Drive Systems takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. Yarrow Drive Systems describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies.
