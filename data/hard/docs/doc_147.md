# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q4 2025

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The company updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. The group kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time. The board discussed returns handling at 21 of its meetings. The management team met customers at 21 regional trade events. The company ran 15 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality.

The group reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers. The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms.

The company sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty. The quality group introduced a shorter checklist for supplier audits, which reduced rework. Maintenance at the service centre was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. The company updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in November.

Yarrow joined an industry working group on shared standards for document control.
