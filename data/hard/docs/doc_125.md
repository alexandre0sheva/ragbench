# Westmark Polymers appoints Verena Ellingham as chief executive

Westmark Polymers announced on 14 September 2019 that its board had appointed Verena Ellingham as chief executive officer with effect from 28 September 2019. Verena Ellingham succeeds Katarin Ostergaard, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Verena Ellingham was previously head of engineering at Vantor Aerial.

Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. Westmark updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it.
