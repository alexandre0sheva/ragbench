# Marisco Devices appoints Theodora Kowalczyk as chief executive

Marisco Devices announced on 3 January 2024 that its board had appointed Theodora Kowalczyk as chief executive officer with effect from 26 January 2024. Theodora Kowalczyk succeeds Pavel Whitlock, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Theodora Kowalczyk was previously finance director at Calder Freight.

Marisco renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms. Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half.
