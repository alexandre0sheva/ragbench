# Calder Freight appoints Talia Blackwood as chief executive

Calder Freight announced on 3 June 2021 that its board had appointed Talia Blackwood as chief executive officer with effect from 11 July 2021. Talia Blackwood succeeds Tamsin Corrigan, who is retiring. Talia Blackwood was previously managing director of the export business at Ashgrove Analytics.

Calder continued to invest in the service centre, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The group updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it.
