# Vantor Aerial: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 20 June 2022.*

Vantor Aerial is a maker of survey and inspection drones. It was founded in 2002 in Merrowgate, where its head office is located. Nikolai Zelenko founded Vantor and served as its first chief executive. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The register lists Vantor as a private limited company. It files its annual return with the register on time. Vantor describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies.
