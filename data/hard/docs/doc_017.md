# Brightwater Instruments appoints Odile Norquist as chief executive

Brightwater Instruments announced on 21 February 2022 that its board had appointed Odile Norquist as chief executive officer with effect from 13 March 2022. Odile Norquist succeeds Rosalind Arkwright, who is retiring. Odile Norquist was previously finance director at Umbra Grid.

The group continued to invest in the service centre, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The training unit reported 39 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.
