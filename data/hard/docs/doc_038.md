# Yarrow Drives appoints Emrys Quarrie as chief executive

Yarrow Drives announced on 11 July 2019 that its board had appointed Emrys Quarrie as chief executive officer with effect from 3 August 2019. Emrys Quarrie succeeds Wendell Jovanovic, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Emrys Quarrie was previously chief operating officer at Tessaline Marine.

Maintenance at the northern depot was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. The group joined an industry working group on shared standards for digital service records.
