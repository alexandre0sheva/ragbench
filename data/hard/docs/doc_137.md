# Orlan Glassworks appoints Hester Dunleavy as chief executive

Orlan Glassworks announced on 6 August 2023 that its board had appointed Hester Dunleavy as chief executive officer with effect from 8 September 2023. Hester Dunleavy succeeds Ivo Calloway, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Hester Dunleavy was previously head of engineering at Westmark Polymers.

Changes to spare-parts planning were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. Spare-parts availability improved after the quality group reorganised the assembly hall.
