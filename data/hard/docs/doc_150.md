# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q2 2025

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the second quarter of 2025.

The quality group repeated its check of shift planning and closed all open actions. The company published a revised supplier handbook in February. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. Work on warehouse layout continued, with most of the planned changes now in place. The engineering office reported 35 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.

The management team met customers at 17 regional trade events. The company expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.

The quality group reported 40 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms. Vantor ran 37 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. The group updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.

The group reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers.
