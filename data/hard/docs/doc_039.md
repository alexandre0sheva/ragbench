# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q3 2024

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the third quarter of 2024.

Internal audit looked at digital service records and reported no significant findings. Norrland joined an industry working group on shared standards for energy metering. The group continued to invest in the service centre, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The management team met customers at 28 regional trade events. The training unit reported 40 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.

The company joined an industry working group on shared standards for spare-parts planning. The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms.

The quality group repeated its check of spare-parts planning and closed all open actions. Norrland updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. Maintenance at the service centre was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. Norrland reviewed its approach to shift planning during the year and simplified several internal steps. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.

The logistics desk took on 9 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers.
