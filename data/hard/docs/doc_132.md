# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q2 2024

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the second quarter of 2024.

Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half. A new scheduling tool now plans work at the main workshop a week ahead. Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. The engineering office introduced a shorter checklist for packaging waste, which reduced rework.

The quality group repeated its check of customer onboarding and closed all open actions. The group renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms.

The quality group repeated its check of warehouse layout and closed all open actions. Vantor continued to invest in the service centre, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. Spare-parts availability improved after the logistics desk reorganised the service centre. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in April. The company reviewed its approach to workshop safety during the year and simplified several internal steps.

The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.
