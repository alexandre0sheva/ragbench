# Lumen Harbor Systems appoints Delphine Sorrell as chief executive

Lumen Harbor Systems announced on 6 May 2020 that its board had appointed Delphine Sorrell as chief executive officer with effect from 18 June 2020. Delphine Sorrell succeeds Brigid Abernathy, who is retiring. Delphine Sorrell was previously head of engineering at Westmark Polymers.

Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in July.
