# Redfern Composites appoints Agathe Mortensen as chief executive

Redfern Composites announced on 21 December 2021 that its board had appointed Agathe Mortensen as chief executive officer with effect from 14 January 2022. Agathe Mortensen succeeds Xavier Tolliver, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Agathe Mortensen was previously managing director of the export business at Kestova Energy.

In September, the engineering office completed a review of packaging waste at the training rooms. Redfern kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time.
