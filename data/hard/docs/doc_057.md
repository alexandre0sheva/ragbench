# Redfern Composites appoints Marten Penhaligon as chief executive

Redfern Composites announced on 24 February 2025 that its board had appointed Marten Penhaligon as chief executive officer with effect from 10 March 2025. Marten Penhaligon succeeds Agathe Mortensen, who is retiring. Marten Penhaligon was previously head of engineering at Vantor Aerial.

Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. The board discussed workshop safety at 17 of its meetings.
