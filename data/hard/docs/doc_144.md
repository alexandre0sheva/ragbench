# Ironvale Sensing: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 21 October 2022.*

Ironvale Sensing is a maker of industrial gas detectors. It was founded in 2015 in Kessrick, where its head office is located. Bastian Ravensworth founded the business. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. It files its annual return with the register on time. Ironvale Sensing takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme.
