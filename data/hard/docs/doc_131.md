# Tessaline Marine: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 10 September 2022.*

Tessaline Marine is a builder of small workboats. It was founded in 2007 in Brennock, where its head office is located. Rhea Falkner founded Tessaline and served as its first chief executive. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The register lists Tessaline as a private limited company. It files its annual return with the register on time. Tessaline takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme.
