# Ostrow Bay harbour tariff, 2025 edition

Issued by the Ostrow Bay Harbour Board. This edition takes effect on 1 July 2025 and replaces the edition of 1 September 2024.

| Charge | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Berth fee, per metre of length per day | MC 3.82 |
| Pilotage, per movement | MC 532 |
| Container handling, per TEU | MC 63 |

Charges are invoiced to the ship's agent after departure. Vessels on scheduled ferry services pay under a separate agreement. Disputes about an invoice must be raised within thirty days.
