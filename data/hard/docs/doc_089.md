# Tessaline Marine appoints Greta Ashdown as chief executive

Tessaline Marine announced on 24 April 2023 that its board had appointed Greta Ashdown as chief executive officer with effect from 7 June 2023. Greta Ashdown succeeds Rhea Falkner, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Greta Ashdown was previously chief operating officer at Brightwater Instruments.

Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in March. The field service team took on 32 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers.
