# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q3 2025

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the third quarter of 2025.

Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. The logistics desk introduced a shorter checklist for calibration routines, which reduced rework. The field service team introduced a shorter checklist for warehouse layout, which reduced rework. Norrland reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers. The group sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty.

The company ran 30 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules.

The group sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty. The company renegotiated several supply agreements to include clearer delivery terms. The group reviewed its approach to shift planning during the year and simplified several internal steps. The procurement office reported 18 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in January.

The company expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.
