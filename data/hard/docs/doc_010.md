# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q2 2024

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the second quarter of 2024.

The company sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty. A pilot of workshop safety at the northern depot ran from July to August and will be extended. Norrland reviewed its approach to supplier audits during the year and simplified several internal steps. Work on shift planning continued, with most of the planned changes now in place. The group kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time.

Maintenance at the coastal test site was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. Changes to customer onboarding were rolled out in stages to limit disruption.
