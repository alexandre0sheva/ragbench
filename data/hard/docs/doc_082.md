# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q1 2025

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the first quarter of 2025.

The quality group repeated its check of digital service records and closed all open actions. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in February. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. A pilot of document control at the service centre ran from February to August and will be extended. The training unit reported 40 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time.

The logistics desk reported 15 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the main workshop was replaced.

A pilot of packaging waste at the main workshop ran from February to July and will be extended. The board discussed shift planning at 24 of its meetings.
