# Ironvale Sensors appoints Silas Yardley as chief executive

Ironvale Sensors announced on 27 October 2023 that its board had appointed Silas Yardley as chief executive officer with effect from 13 November 2023. Silas Yardley succeeds Kaia Nyberg, who is retiring. Silas Yardley was previously managing director of the export business at Hessle Motorworks.

Changes to workshop safety were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.
