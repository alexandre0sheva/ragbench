# Calder Freight appoints Tamsin Corrigan as chief executive

Calder Freight announced on 18 April 2020 that its board had appointed Tamsin Corrigan as chief executive officer with effect from 1 June 2020. Tamsin Corrigan succeeds Leopold Fontaine, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Tamsin Corrigan was previously head of engineering at Redfern Composites.

The group continued to invest in the northern depot, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The management team met customers at 28 regional trade events.
