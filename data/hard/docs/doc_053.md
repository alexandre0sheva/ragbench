# Norrland Battery Works: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 9 June 2022.*

Norrland Battery Works is a recycler of industrial batteries. It was founded in 1989 in Valtessa, where its head office is located. Desmond Rasmussen founded the business. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop. The register lists Norrland Battery Works as a private limited company.
