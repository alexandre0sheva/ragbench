# Lumen Harbor Systems moves its head office

Lumen Harbor Systems completed the move of its head office from Halbrook to Dunmore Reach on 19 March 2025. The former Halbrook office will stay open as a regional service depot. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules.

In September, the engineering office completed a review of digital service records at the main workshop. The procurement office introduced a shorter checklist for spare-parts planning, which reduced rework.
