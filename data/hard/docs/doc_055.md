# Yarrow Drives appoints Annika Lysander as chief executive

Yarrow Drives announced on 11 July 2023 that its board had appointed Annika Lysander as chief executive officer with effect from 3 August 2023. Annika Lysander succeeds Emrys Quarrie, who is retiring. Annika Lysander was previously chief operating officer at Tessaline Marine.

Yarrow updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. A new scheduling tool now plans work at the training rooms a week ahead.
