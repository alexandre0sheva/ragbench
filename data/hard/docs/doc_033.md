# Norrland Batteries: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 1 July 2022.*

Norrland Batteries is a maker of marine battery packs. It was founded in 1987 in Ostrow Bay, where its head office is located. Zora Drummond founded Norrland and served as its first chief executive. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. The register lists Norrland as a private limited company. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams.
