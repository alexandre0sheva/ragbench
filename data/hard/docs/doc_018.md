# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q4 2024

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the fourth quarter of 2024.

A pilot of supplier audits at the training rooms ran from April to December and will be extended. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform. The company kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time. In July, the field service team completed a review of returns handling at the assembly hall. Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms.

The facilities team reported 17 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. The quality group repeated its check of calibration routines and closed all open actions.

Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. The group ran 17 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. The group updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. Spare-parts availability improved after the field service team reorganised the coastal test site.

Norrland expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.
