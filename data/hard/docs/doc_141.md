# Ashgrove Analytics appoints Maren Wexford as chief executive

Ashgrove Analytics announced on 29 June 2023 that its board had appointed Maren Wexford as chief executive officer with effect from 9 August 2023. Maren Wexford succeeds Uriel Vandermeer, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Maren Wexford was previously managing director of the export business at Quarry Point Robotics.

Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half. The group reviewed its approach to shift planning during the year and simplified several internal steps.
