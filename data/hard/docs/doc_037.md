# Hessle Motorworks appoints Stellan Ingersen as chief executive

Hessle Motorworks announced on 20 November 2020 that its board had appointed Stellan Ingersen as chief executive officer with effect from 7 December 2020. Stellan Ingersen succeeds Mireille Gallagher, who is retiring. Stellan Ingersen was previously head of engineering at Pellucid Optics.

The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform. Hessle continued to invest in the northern depot, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair.
