# Norrland Batteries appoints Ottilie Fairweather as chief executive

Norrland Batteries announced on 8 February 2021 that its board had appointed Ottilie Fairweather as chief executive officer with effect from 12 March 2021. Ottilie Fairweather succeeds Zora Drummond, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Ottilie Fairweather was previously managing director of the export business at Marisco Devices.

The board discussed shift planning at 37 of its meetings. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.
