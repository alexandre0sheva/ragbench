# Hessle Motorworks: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 12 February 2022.*

Hessle Motorworks is a maker of electric drive motors. It was founded in 2001 in Merrowgate, where its head office is located. Ragna Okafor founded Hessle and served as its first chief executive. The register lists Hessle as a private limited company. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Hessle describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies.
