# Zephyrine Cables appoints Isadora Quennell as chief executive

Zephyrine Cables announced on 28 July 2023 that its board had appointed Isadora Quennell as chief executive officer with effect from 2 September 2023. Isadora Quennell succeeds Bertil Ivanova, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Isadora Quennell was previously finance director at Calder Freight.

Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half. In April, the engineering office completed a review of document control at the training rooms.
