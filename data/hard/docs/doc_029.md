# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q4 2025

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The field service team reported 23 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. The customer support desk took on 6 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers. Investment plans focus on packaging waste and on energy metering. The training unit took on 20 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. The company updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. Vantor expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace. Changes to workshop safety were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. The company reviewed its approach to supplier audits during the year and simplified several internal steps.

The company ran 30 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality.
