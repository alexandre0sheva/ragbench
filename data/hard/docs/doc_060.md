# Norrland Batteries quarterly operations report, Q1 2024

This report covers the operations of Norrland Batteries in the first quarter of 2024.

Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. The quality group repeated its check of digital service records and closed all open actions. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in May. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform. Work on shift planning continued, with most of the planned changes now in place.

The quality group repeated its check of workshop safety and closed all open actions. Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes.

The group ran 4 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. Changes to spare-parts planning were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. The company continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in February. Work on energy metering continued, with most of the planned changes now in place.

The group reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers.
