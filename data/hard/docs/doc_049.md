# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q3 2025

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the third quarter of 2025.

In April, the field service team completed a review of supplier audits at the assembly hall. The group continued to invest in the coastal test site, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. In June, the procurement office completed a review of energy metering at the main workshop. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. The group expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.

The board discussed document control at 26 of its meetings. The procurement office took on 20 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers.

The procurement office introduced a shorter checklist for spare-parts planning, which reduced rework. Vantor ran 37 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules. The board discussed calibration routines at 6 of its meetings. The management team met customers at 24 regional trade events.

Investment plans focus on customer onboarding and on packaging waste.
