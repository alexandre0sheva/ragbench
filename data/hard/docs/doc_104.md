# Kestova Energy appoints Cordelia Kilbride as chief executive

Kestova Energy announced on 23 May 2022 that its board had appointed Cordelia Kilbride as chief executive officer with effect from 22 June 2022. Cordelia Kilbride succeeds Vesna Holmqvist, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Cordelia Kilbride was previously managing director of the export business at Westmark Polymers.

Spare-parts availability improved after the quality group reorganised the service centre. Kestova published a revised supplier handbook in March.
