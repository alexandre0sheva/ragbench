# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q3 2025

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the third quarter of 2025.

The group expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace. Maintenance at the service centre was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand. The management team met customers at 12 regional trade events. The company sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty. Yarrow joined an industry working group on shared standards for warehouse layout.

Changes to packaging waste were rolled out in stages to limit disruption. Maintenance at the assembly hall was moved to quieter weeks to avoid peak demand.

The company reviewed its approach to supplier audits during the year and simplified several internal steps. The quality group repeated its check of shift planning and closed all open actions. The group updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it. The group published a revised supplier handbook in June. The field service team took on 40 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers.

Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in March.
