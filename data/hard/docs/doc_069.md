# Pellucid Optics appoints Casimir Underhill as chief executive

Pellucid Optics announced on 6 May 2021 that its board had appointed Casimir Underhill as chief executive officer with effect from 12 June 2021. Casimir Underhill succeeds Elodie Stranack, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Casimir Underhill was previously finance director at Yarrow Drives.

In January, the facilities team completed a review of returns handling at the coastal test site. Pellucid kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time.
