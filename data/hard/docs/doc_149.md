# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q4 2024

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Vantor continued to invest in the main workshop, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The group continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. The quality group repeated its check of digital service records and closed all open actions. The quality group repeated its check of customer onboarding and closed all open actions. Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the training rooms was replaced.

Investment plans focus on shift planning and on customer onboarding. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases.
