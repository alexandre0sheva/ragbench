# Westmark Polymers: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 18 March 2022.*

Westmark Polymers is a maker of polymer housings and hull parts. It was founded in 2003 in Merrowgate, where its head office is located. Katarin Ostergaard founded Westmark and served as its first chief executive. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. Westmark describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. Westmark takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. It files its annual return with the register on time.
