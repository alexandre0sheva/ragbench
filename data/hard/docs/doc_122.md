# Redfern Composites: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 15 August 2022.*

Redfern Composites is a maker of composite hull shells. It was founded in 2010 in Brennock, where its head office is located. Xavier Tolliver founded Redfern and served as its first chief executive. Redfern takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. Its staff are organised into engineering, operations and customer support teams. The register lists Redfern as a private limited company. It files its annual return with the register on time.
