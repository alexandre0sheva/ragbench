# Ashgrove Analytics: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 14 March 2022.*

Ashgrove Analytics is a provider of fleet analytics software. It was founded in 1994 in Kessrick, where its head office is located. Florian Thackeray founded Ashgrove and served as its first chief executive. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Ashgrove describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop.
