# Umbra Grid: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 26 August 2022.*

Umbra Grid is an operator of harbour power grids. It was founded in 1986 in Ostrow Bay, where its head office is located. Nils Jessop founded Umbra and served as its first chief executive. It files its annual return with the register on time. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Umbra takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme.
