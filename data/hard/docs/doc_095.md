# Pellucid Optics: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 10 February 2022.*

Pellucid Optics is a maker of camera lens assemblies. It was founded in 1993 in Brennock, where its head office is located. Elodie Stranack founded Pellucid and served as its first chief executive. It files its annual return with the register on time. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Pellucid takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme.
