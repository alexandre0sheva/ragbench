# Brightwater Instruments appoints Rosalind Arkwright as chief executive

Brightwater Instruments announced on 11 August 2020 that its board had appointed Rosalind Arkwright as chief executive officer with effect from 25 August 2020. Rosalind Arkwright succeeds Percival Kerrigan, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Rosalind Arkwright was previously managing director of the export business at Pellucid Optics.

A new scheduling tool now plans work at the northern depot a week ahead. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases.
