# Vantor Aerial appoints Ubaldo Nakamura as chief executive

Vantor Aerial announced on 15 July 2023 that its board had appointed Ubaldo Nakamura as chief executive officer with effect from 30 July 2023. Ubaldo Nakamura succeeds Nikolai Zelenko, who will join the board as a non-executive director. Ubaldo Nakamura was previously chief operating officer at Norrland Batteries.

Internal audit looked at supplier audits and reported no significant findings. Vantor reviewed its insurance cover and kept the main policies with existing providers.
