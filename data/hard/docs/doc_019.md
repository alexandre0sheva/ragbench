# Umbra Grid completes acquisition of Norrland Batteries

Umbra Grid completed its acquisition of Norrland Batteries on 6 August 2025, after approval by the Meridian Coast Competition Office. Norrland Batteries keeps its name and its own management team. The parties did not disclose the price.

Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. Internal audit looked at calibration routines and reported no significant findings.
