# Sablewood Logistics moves its head office

Sablewood Logistics completed the move of its head office from Merrowgate to Dunmore Reach on 2 May 2024. The former Merrowgate office will stay open as a regional service depot. Lead times for standard orders shortened slightly over the second half.

Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms. The company updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it.
