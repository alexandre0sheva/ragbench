# Zephyrine Cables appoints Fenna Grisholm as chief executive

Zephyrine Cables announced on 10 October 2024 that its board had appointed Fenna Grisholm as chief executive officer with effect from 14 November 2024. Fenna Grisholm succeeds Isadora Quennell, who is leaving to pursue other interests. Fenna Grisholm was previously managing director of the export business at Calder Freight.

A new scheduling tool now plans work at the assembly hall a week ahead. The finance team moved its monthly reporting to a shared platform.
