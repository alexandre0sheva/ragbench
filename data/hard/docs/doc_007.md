# Yarrow Drives: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 26 May 2022.*

Yarrow Drives is a maker of electric drive motors. It was founded in 2008 in Valtessa, where its head office is located. Wendell Jovanovic founded Yarrow and served as its first chief executive. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop. Most of its revenue comes from long-term service agreements rather than one-off sales. Yarrow takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. The register lists Yarrow as a private limited company.
