# Vantor Aerial quarterly operations report, Q3 2024

This report covers the operations of Vantor Aerial in the third quarter of 2024.

Work on calibration routines continued, with most of the planned changes now in place. Investment plans focus on energy metering and on customer onboarding. Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. The group continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements. Work on warehouse layout continued, with most of the planned changes now in place.

Demand from port operators remained steady, while survey and inspection customers ordered more often but in smaller volumes. Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms.

The procurement office introduced a shorter checklist for customer onboarding, which reduced rework. The quality group repeated its check of packaging waste and closed all open actions. The group ran 38 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality. The group continued to invest in the training rooms, replacing older equipment where it was no longer economical to repair. The group published a revised supplier handbook in July.

The group expects the market for coastal monitoring to keep growing at a moderate pace.
