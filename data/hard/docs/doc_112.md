# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q4 2024

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in April. The group kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time. A pilot of warehouse layout at the assembly hall ran from May to November and will be extended. Several long-standing customers renewed their service agreements on similar terms. Training hours per employee rose, mainly because of new safety modules.

Energy use per unit produced fell after the lighting at the service centre was replaced. The company updated its code of conduct and asked every employee to confirm they had read it.
