# Calder Freight: company profile

*Profile prepared by the Meridian Coast Business Register, last reviewed 16 February 2022.*

Calder Freight is a coastal freight operator. It was founded in 2000 in Ferrisvale, where its head office is located. Leopold Fontaine founded Calder and served as its first chief executive. Calder describes its customers as port operators, survey firms and public agencies. Calder takes part in the Meridian Coast apprenticeship scheme. The company holds a quality certificate for its main workshop. The company publishes a short sustainability statement each spring.
