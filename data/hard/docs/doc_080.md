# Yarrow Drives quarterly operations report, Q1 2025

This report covers the operations of Yarrow Drives in the first quarter of 2025.

The customer support desk reported 33 minor incidents, none of which caused lost working time. Work on workshop safety continued, with most of the planned changes now in place. Internal audit looked at packaging waste and reported no significant findings. The group kept its customer satisfaction survey unchanged so that results can be compared over time. The company ran 4 workshops with customers to collect feedback on service quality.

The training unit introduced a shorter checklist for digital service records, which reduced rework. Yarrow continued to support local schools with workshop visits and summer placements.

Feedback from the employee survey was shared with every team in September. Freight costs were broadly stable, although winter storms delayed a number of coastal deliveries. Spare-parts availability improved after the facilities team reorganised the northern depot. The quality group repeated its check of energy metering and closed all open actions. The logistics desk took on 18 new colleagues, most of them through internal transfers.

The group sees exchange-rate movements and coastal weather as the main sources of uncertainty.
