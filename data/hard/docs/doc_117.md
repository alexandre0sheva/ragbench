# Sablewood Logistics appoints Ilse Ysselstein as chief executive

Sablewood Logistics announced on 9 February 2020 that its board had appointed Ilse Ysselstein as chief executive officer with effect from 23 February 2020. Ilse Ysselstein succeeds Piet Halloway, who is retiring. Ilse Ysselstein was previously head of engineering at Norrland Batteries.

Customer complaints were handled within the target time in most cases. Work on supplier audits continued, with most of the planned changes now in place.
